# Power_BI_Project 1
UBER BOOKING DATA ANALYSIS REPORT
Prepared by: Tanvi Desai
Date: 31 August 2025

**Summary**
This project analyses a dataset of 150,000 Uber ride bookings to uncover insights into customer behaviour, operational efficiency, and payment preferences. Using Power BI for data transformation, visualization, and dashboarding, the analysis highlights key trends in ride demand, cancellation patterns, and satisfaction drivers.
Key Takeaways:
•	Peak booking hours: 5 PM – 7 PM, suggesting strong evening commute demand.
•	Most used vehicle type: Auto, followed by Go Mini and Go Sedan.
•	Top payment method: UPI, indicating strong digital payment adoption.
•	Major cancellation reasons: Wrong pickup locations and driver delays.
•	Service quality, not fare or distance, drives customer satisfaction.

**Conclusion**
This analysis reveals that Uber’s operational efficiency and user experience can be significantly improved through:
•	Enhanced pickup accuracy and communication,
•	Optimized driver allocation during peak hours, and
•	Focus on customer service quality as the key satisfaction driver.

# Power_BI_Project 2
AIRLINE FLIGHT PRICE ANALYSIS REPORT
Prepared by: Tanvi Desai
Date: 15th August 2025

**Executive Summary**
This project explores flight pricing patterns across multiple Indian cities and airlines to understand how timing, class, and booking behaviour influence ticket prices. Using Power BI for data cleaning, transformation, and visualization, this analysis reveals pricing dynamics and operational insights that could help both airlines and travellers make informed decisions.
Key Insights:
•	Airline pricing varies significantly, with premium carriers such as Vistara and Air India commanding higher average fares.
•	Business class tickets are consistently priced higher than Economy, but the premium fluctuates depending on city pairs.
•	Early bookings result in lower fares — prices rise steeply as days left to departure decrease.
•	Departure and arrival time influence prices: evening and late-night flights tend to be more expensive due to higher demand.
•	Non-stop flights command premium prices compared to connecting ones.

**Conclusion**
The analysis shows that flight ticket pricing depends on multiple interrelated factors:
•	Time sensitivity (days left to departure),
•	Service level (class type), and
•	Route preference (non-stop vs connecting).
These findings illustrate how data analytics and visualization in Power BI can uncover actionable insights from airline datasets to optimize both pricing strategy and customer decision-making.
