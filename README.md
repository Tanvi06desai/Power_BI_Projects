UBER BOOKING DATA ANALYSIS REPORT
Prepared by: Tanvi Desai
Date: 31 August 2025
________________________________________

**Summary**
This project analyses a dataset of 150,000 Uber ride bookings to uncover insights into customer behaviour, operational efficiency, and payment preferences. Using Power BI for data transformation, visualization, and dashboarding, the analysis highlights key trends in ride demand, cancellation patterns, and satisfaction drivers.
Key Takeaways:
•	Peak booking hours: 5 PM – 7 PM, suggesting strong evening commute demand.
•	Most used vehicle type: Auto, followed by Go Mini and Go Sedan.
•	Top payment method: UPI, indicating strong digital payment adoption.
•	Major cancellation reasons: Wrong pickup locations and driver delays.
•	Service quality, not fare or distance, drives customer satisfaction.

**Conclusion**
This analysis reveals that Uber’s operational efficiency and user experience can be significantly improved through:
•	Enhanced pickup accuracy and communication,
•	Optimized driver allocation during peak hours, and
•	Focus on customer service quality as the key satisfaction driver.
